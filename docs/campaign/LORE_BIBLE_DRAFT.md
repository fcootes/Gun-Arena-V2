## 1. Creative identity and scope

ZERO DIRECTIVE is a cinematic military-science-fiction action thriller with controlled survival-horror escalation, realistic-feeling squad tactics, advanced military technology, and a corporate-government conspiracy. The desired tonal balance is approximately **70% military action / 30% science-fiction horror**, varying by chapter. The supernatural-feeling events must not displace the human conflict.

Influence references are **tone/structure benchmarks only, not material to reproduce**: *Halo* (action and restrained heroes, large-scale military presentation, major enemy revelation, escalating two-sided conflict); *Resident Evil: Infinite Darkness* (short cinematic helicopter insertion, ominous scientific mystery and enhanced adversary); *Star Wars* (an unseen manipulator and an intimidating field enforcer). All assets, characters, environments, scripts, audio and silhouettes must be original.

**Provisional scope:** **eight missions, five major environments, roughly 20 minutes per mission**. These are pacing targets, not commitments or measured runtimes. Each mission should justify its length through varied objectives, discovery, navigation, tactical encounters, squad moments and a proper extraction or resolution—not waves of filler enemies.

**Mode transition (approved direction):** the current *Extraction* foundation eventually becomes a distinct, flagship **Campaign** experience with its own presentation, Continue/New Campaign/Mission Select and persistence. FFA, Team, Horde/Survival and Training Grounds remain separate, intact modes. Multiplayer is a distant possibility, not an active deliverable. Reuse functioning extraction/world systems where appropriate without treating the current Extraction lobby as the final Campaign UI.

**Cinematic rule:** around 30 seconds of skippable authored insertion for major missions, transitioning smoothly into first-person control. Develop actual cinematics after the agreed graphics, animation and gameplay foundations; design narrative requirements now.

## 2. Story foundation: approved directions

- **The original government programme:** A classified research/human-enhancement initiative studied a highly dangerous phenomenon known as **Strain Zero**. The government officially discontinued it because of unacceptable risk. Most government officials do **not** know it continued.
- **The hidden continuation:** A powerful technology/defence consortium and a small number of corrupt officials continue the work covertly across private, military and research facilities. The **Architect** manipulates government and corporate interests, exploiting their rivalry and compartmentalised information.
- **Strain Zero's origin:** It is *actually extraterrestrial*, but ZERO DIRECTIVE **must only hint** at this; there is no explicit on-screen or dialogue confirmation in the first game. Definitive origin belongs, if ever, to a potential sequel. How it first arrived on Earth is **unresolved**.
- **Area 51 cause and effect:** APEX's assault/operation exploits an already under-secured facility and partially causes a catastrophic containment failure. **APEX Area 51 takes place before USMC Area 51.** The player must not see or conclusively identify infected creatures in the APEX mission; odd warnings, strange sounds and incomplete evidence can foreshadow them. The later Marine mission is the **first on-screen infected revelation**, preferably **Mission 4**.
- **The Architect:** Intellectually formidable, calculated and intentionally ruthless. His central ambitions are growing personal and institutional power, comprehensive control, self-optimisation and potentially immortality. His true identity and involvement should be revealed **late**, with his influence felt earlier. He survives the first game's conclusion.
- **GHOST commander:** A highly enhanced **human** elite commander, loyal to the Architect, who serves as his intimidating field enforcer. The desired *relationship archetype* is a mastermind with a feared subordinate, not copied characters. He should be encountered repeatedly, first as an overwhelming presence rather than an early boss. The planned climax defeats him and stops the immediate ZERO DIRECTIVE threat; whether he definitively dies or survives is **still open**.
- **Abomination:** A failed attempt to modify/enhance a previously elite special-operations soldier. An imposing, original non-graphic sci-fi design, visually retaining a trace of military history. The Abomination is a consequence of the programme, **not** the GHOST commander or the Architect.
- **Two playable recurring protagonists:** One USMC, one APEX; both have real biography, squad relationships and limited but impactful dialogue. **Names and detailed arcs remain open.**
- **Rainer:** Earlier planning established him as a recurring signature Marine supporting character, used selectively rather than permanently assigned to every level. Preserve the concept; do not invent unapproved rank/biography/dialogue.
- **APEX's collapse:** The Architect/Directorate has secretly cultivated GHOST as a more controllable replacement for APEX, whose soldiers put loyalty to comrades above corporate command. APEX and GHOST receive overlapping orders on a morally objectionable mission (railway is the preferred location). Disagreement escalates into armed conflict, exposing the secret replacement plan. **APEX fragments**: breakaway operators reject the Directorate; loyalists continue to serve it; GHOST stays directly and absolutely loyal to the Architect.
- **Alliance:** USMC unexpectedly encounters APEX fighting GHOST, initially suspects a hostile quarrel, then forms a **wary operational alliance** with breakaway APEX. The alliance must be earned through action and incomplete trust, not instant friendship. An early appearance of the GHOST commander fits this encounter. Loyalist APEX may still be encountered outside Area 51 in the subsequent USMC mission.

## 5. Five-environment campaign plan

1. **Area 51:** Established playable facility with four zones (staging, decontamination, bio-lab, upper warehouse/extraction). Expand to desert exterior, arrival site and credible access from the surface. APEX pre-catastrophe and USMC aftermath are different authored scenarios. Marine insertion at **dusk transitioning toward night**, with increasing darkness deeper inside. Huge ventilation fan, unsettling silence, believable evidence of emergency. First infected *seen* by Marine player only.
2. **Railway / freight complex** (*Iron Vein* working label): freight yards, warehouses, trains and maintenance infrastructure. Ideal site for morally disputed overlapping APEX/GHOST orders and escalating firefight, USMC arrival and uneasy cooperation. Primarily grounded human conflict, **not** a premature infected reveal.
3. **City** (*Glasshouse* working label): focused tactical city district with corporate linkages, streets, interiors and societal consequences of the conspiracy; not a giant open world. Mission objectives/chronology TBD.
4. **Operation Shattered Wall / Pacific Rim:** Existing stormy helipad and catwalk map grows into a **scientific research installation disguised as a functioning offshore oil rig**. Multi-deck operations, industrial, crew/service, concealed research and lower maintenance levels. Arrive via distinctive mission-specific insertion. The facility's relationship to Strain Zero is important, but exact discovery/provenance is intentionally unresolved to preserve sequel mystery.
5. **Directorate military installation / command site:** **Preferred fifth environment, not final approval.** A defended corporate-military facility able to support the final showdown, evidence recovery and attempted ZERO DIRECTIVE execution. The older draft called the fifth location a Blacksite; this may evolve into a military installation with a hidden research/command archive. Exact location, art and story function TBD.

**No sixth environment approved.** Reuse a location for distinct faction missions only when objectives, route, faction position and narrative information differ meaningfully.

## 7. Recommended revelation and pacing rules

- **Human conflict first.** Establish APEX, Directorate, USMC and their competing objectives before supernatural-feeling threats.
- **Foreshadow without spoiling.** In the pre-incident APEX facility use restricted warnings, unexplained alarms, unreliable reports, closed sector markers and suspicious sounds. No definitive infected silhouettes, boss introductions or character statements giving away the reveal.
- **Marine Area 51 first contact:** initial desert/exterior exploration, limited confrontation with stranded or loyalist APEX, security discovery, atmospheric industrial fan/distant sounds and a credible survivor or recovered message; the first infected arrival is a deliberate, distinctive narrative/AI set piece, not a routine spawn.
- **Dusk-to-night transition:** Dusk helicopter flight and surface arrival; increasingly dark, controlled lighting inside the underground research zones.
- **Action/horror contrast:** Alternate human firefights, navigation, investigation, industrial hazards, squad choices and outbreak-focused encounters. Do not use infected hordes in every mission.
- **Mission insertion:** ~30 seconds, skippable, smooth hand-off; unique for each mission; build after gameplay/graphics foundation.
- **Replayability:** Different difficulty tactics, route options, selective variable encounter arrangements, optional intelligence/squad tasks and meaningful checkpoint/restart. No artificial mandatory waiting to inflate 20-minute runtime.
- **Mode distinction:** Campaign uses authored narrative objective chains and progression. Horde/Survival, FFA, Team and Training Grounds preserve their own functional rules and maps.

## 8. The title ZERO DIRECTIVE — meaning not yet approved

**Proposal:** ZERO DIRECTIVE is the Architect's top-level contingency protocol, superseding regular Directorate/GHOST operating rules, securing or suppressing the consortium's secret programme and treating disloyal forces as liabilities. Its precise order, moral consequences, activation and how the heroes stop it **remain open**. Do not assume this version is final canon.

## 9. Ending direction and sequel boundaries

**Approved outcome:** The protagonists stop the immediate ZERO DIRECTIVE threat and defeat the GHOST commander; the Architect survives. The first game's principal crisis must have a satisfying conclusion.

**Still open:** Whether GHOST commander survives or dies, how the conspirators are exposed, exact final mission location, whether APEX breakaway becomes autonomous and whether protagonists cooperate openly or remain pragmatic rivals.

**Sequel seed:** Strain Zero's extraterrestrial origin may be confirmed later; first game includes hints only. No definitive alien reveal, visiting spacecraft or explicit explanation in dialogue.
